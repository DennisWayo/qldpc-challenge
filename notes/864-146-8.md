## What was searched

From github.com/QEC-pages/Quantum_LDPC_Codes @ 1c95489383564e4dc2cce517de00d64d6f2c4f56, `Hyperbolic_Codes_Planar.zip`, this code is the {4,6} planar hyperbolic code with 864 qubits. These are incidence matrices of a finite quotient of the regular {4,6} hyperbolic tiling (edge model).
