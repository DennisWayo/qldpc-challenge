# [[684,20,72]] — Stage 1 non-cyclic group-algebra search

## Direction & hypothesis

Stage 1 targeted the unrestricted weight-8 cell. The search used a two-block
group-algebra CSS construction and kept only candidates whose maximum check
weight was at most 8. The construction metadata is:

`two-block-group-algebra CSS construction over semidirect(19,18,2); with A=[[25,136,177,278]]; and B=[[35,233,240,309]]; generated by the non-cyclic Stage 1 search.`

The search objective was to exceed the Stage 1 reference score
`66` while preserving CSS commutation, the claimed logical
dimension, and the weight cap.

## What was searched

The phylogenetic search ran 386 campaigns and passed candidates through the
native RIS screening and validation queue. Candidate bytes were frozen before
validation. The promoted candidate has n=684, k=20, 342
X checks, 342 Z checks, and maximum check weight 8.

## Evidence trail

The distance values below are witness-backed upper bounds, not exact-distance
claims. The independent `cpp_fast.css_ris_parallel` validation ran
20,000 trials per side and observed:

| Z | 72 | 1039910778483 | 392 |

The submission therefore reports d ≤ 72, with X ≤ 74 and
Z ≤ 72. The validation receipt records `status=clean_budget_complete`
and totals of 20,000,000 X trials and
20,000,000 Z trials. The witnesses in
`codes/684-20-72.json` are the exact supports from that receipt.

## Dead ends and safeguards

The initial screen metadata claimed d=102. That value was not promoted: the
20M validation found the lighter Z witness of weight 72 and an X witness of
weight 74. The submission uses the validated upper bound d=72, avoiding an
overstated distance claim. No exact certification or literature novelty claim
is made.
