## Direction & hypothesis

Target the unrestricted weight-6 frontier for issue #1155 by reconstructing a compact published code absent from the board at this parameter point and check weight. This is a known Lin-Pryadko construction, not a claim of literature novelty. Its n=132, w=5, and claimed d=12 lie inside the hackathon eligibility box.
