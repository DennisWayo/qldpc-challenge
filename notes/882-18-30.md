# [[882,18,30]] — weight-8 open-boundary planar (tile) code on a 21x21 lattice

## Direction & hypothesis

Target cell: `local-2d-bilayer x weight-8`, the weight-8 planar tile family whose
published bar is `kd^2/n ≈ 12.7` (the ILP-exact [[512,18,19]], arXiv:2504.09171).
The board now carries the same family at larger lattices, led by
[[922,18,31]] (`kd^2/n = 18.761`, `notes/924-18-31.md`) on a 21x22 lattice.

The opening this submission takes is the **small-n end of that family**. For
this bulk tile `k = 18` at every lattice size, so `kd^2/n = 9 (d/L)^2`: two
members of the family at the same `k` trade `n` against `d` and can both be
Pareto-non-dominated. The board had the large-n member (924/922 qubits, `d = 31`)
but not a smaller-n sibling with the same tile.

Reduction was checked first. Every board code was scanned for qubits lying in
exactly one check (the `r = 1` graft, which preserves `k`): 273 of 593 have
them, but they are the `d = 3` dense-packed-surface family, where the graft
collapses the distance. The tile entries on the board were already reduced to a
fixpoint (`notes/566-18-20.md`). No board entry offered a safe reduction win.

## What was searched

* The B=4 tile family (`research/local2d/boundary_engine.py::build_planar`)
  across `L = 14..23`, both square and rectangular, with the base support
  `Sf = {(0,0),(0,3),(2,2),(3,0)}`, `Sg = {(0,1),(1,1),(2,0),(3,3)}` — the tile of
  `codes/578-18-20.json`.
* The two-support-swap tile of `codes/924-18-31.json`,
  `Sg = {(0,2),(1,3),(2,0),(3,3)}`, evaluated on the smaller lattice 21x21
  (`n = 882`) and on 22x22, 22x20 and 20x22.
* 300 random weight-8 tiles (4+4 supports in a 4x4 box) screened at `L = 13`,
  and 105 one-support-swap mutants of the B=4 tile screened at `L = 17` and
  `L = 21`.

## Evidence trail

Submitted code: `build_planar(21, 21, Sf, Sg)` with the two-swap `Sg`; exact
GF(2) rank gives `n = 882`, `k = 18`, max check weight 8, interaction radius
4.2426 at 2 layers -> `local-2d-bilayer`. The engine's cleanup removes no qubit.

Confirmation ladder (RIS, lightest logical found; fresh seeds each row):

| budget | seeds | lightest |
|---|---|---|
| 1M | 51, 52, 53 | 32, 32, **30** |
| 3M | 54 | **30** |
| 6M | 61, 62, 63 | 31, **30**, **30** |
| 10M | 64 | **30** |

The lightest value found over all runs is **30**, and no budget in this ladder
reached 29. Claim: witness-backed **upper bound `d ≤ 30`** (the weight-30 Z-side
witness is in the submission; the X side records 45); no exact (`d =`) claim.

Near-misses and collapses:

* **22x22 (`n = 968`) is a screening trap.** At 1M trials over three seeds it
  reads 34, 33, 34; combined with the board's own deep record for that lattice
  (32 at 2M, 31 at 8M and 32M in `notes/924-18-31.md`) the honest value is 31,
  i.e. `kd^2/n = 17.87`, *below* the 21x22 sibling. It was not submitted.
* 21x23 and 23x21 (`n = 966`) read 32 at 1M over three seeds -> 19.08, below
  the 21x21 point's `n`-trade.
* 21x22 with the base tile reads 27 over three seeds at 1M -> 14.88; the
  two-swap tile is strictly better at the same lattice.
* Random weight-8 tiles: only 17 of 300 were viable (`k >= 4`, weight <= 8,
  CSS), best `kd^2/n = 6.59` at `L = 13`.

## Dead ends

* **Low-trial screening at `n ≈ 900` is inflated by 2-4**, so mutant ranking at
  30k or 100k trials is unusable: every mutant that beat the base tile at those
  budgets collapsed below it at 1M. The 22x22 row above is the clearest case
  and is why the submitted claim is the 6M-budget value, not the 1M one.
* **`research/local2d/transfer.py::distance_slope` does not scale here.** Its
  transfer graph is exponential in the row-axis degree, and a 4+4-term tile has
  max x-degree 4, so no axis passes the `Dcap` filter.
* **Rectangular distortion does not pay**: `(19,26)` and `(26,19)` gave 24/23 at
  100k, and `(20,22)`-class rectangles were already recorded by
  `notes/924-18-31.md` as losing to the near-square ones.
* **Deeper reduction of the family is not free.** The documented row-space
  qubit removal moved 578 -> 566 previously and 924 -> 922 on the board, at
  unchanged `k` and with the distance held; it buys at most a couple of qubits,
  which does not move `kd^2/n` past the next lattice rung.

## Tools

Model `DeepSeek V4 Flash 0731`. numpy plus the repo's bit-packed RIS
(`verify/gf2_fast`); construction and layout from
`research/local2d/boundary_engine.py` and `research/local2d/planar.py`
(`grid_coordinates`). Roughly 4 hours of wall clock on 16 cores.
